# Karevia Studio · product scope
An iPhone-first editing desk for Turkish and English creators. Start with footage already owned by the user, compose short cuts, or send a generation request to a paired private workstation. No credits, subscriptions, public feed or implicit cloud uploads.

Reference: [Buzzy's public catalog](https://www.buzzy.now/) advertises film, animation, image and video models, music and advertising workflows. This matrix is our own product plan, not a claim that all modes ship today. No Buzzy integrations or assets.

|Capability|Target|Acceptance example|
|---|---|---|
|Text to video|MVP local lab|Worker yields a decodable moving clip from a prompt.|
|Image to video|MVP local lab|Rights-cleared input conditions output; unsupported hardware reports unavailability.|
|AI Film|V1|Editable shot list, approved generation for each shot, cut assembly.|
|Storyboard|V1|Edit scene order and regenerate a single shot without losing the others.|
|UGC ads|V1|User's product photo and approved copy produce a short ad; no impersonation.|
|AI Influencer|Later|Verifiable consent, fictional/authorized identity and abuse controls.|
|AI Animation|Later|Motion guided by a licensed character image.|
|Music video|V1|User-owned audio, editable timing, synchronized export.|
|Video editing|MVP|Trim owned media and play the exported result offline on iPhone.|
|Image generation|Later|Produce and save a licensed-model still.|
|Text to speech|Later|Licensed neutral voices; no voice cloning.|
|AI subtitles|V1|Transcribe locally, correct timings and text, export.|
|Prompt enhancement|V1|User reviews local suggestion before generation.|
|Project library|MVP|Project persists across reopening, deletions remove its media.|
|Export/share|MVP|Playable H.264 MP4, system share action.|

**Feasibility before implementation:** local editing and a single-owner queue fit disk/CPU constraints. Measured VRAM is 8151 MiB; availability or speed of video inference cannot be inferred from model size. Production public inference, App Store signing and device QA require separate evidence. Defer public AI launch unless capacity, uptime, identity and moderation can be sustained for zero additional service charges. No unimplemented modes in store claims.
